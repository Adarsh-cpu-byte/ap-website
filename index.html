About Me

I am a graduate of IIT Delhi with a deep interest in exploring the grey area where politics and policy intersect. Having worked closely with both the political and policy ecosystems of India, I am particularly fascinated by how policy shapes politics—and how, in turn, politics influences policy.

Over time, I have developed a strong interest in the development of Bihar, often regarded as the poorest state in the country. It is also my home state. I was born and raised in Patna, and my ancestral village, Barahiya, is as picturesque as one can imagine, with its flowing river, lush green fields, and a small forest.

Through this platform, I hope to share my thoughts and engage with like-minded individuals who are equally passionate about policy, politics, and regional development.
